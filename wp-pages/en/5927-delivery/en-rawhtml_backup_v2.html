Same-day Catering and Important Delivery Notes

  • Receiving your order: Please make sure someone is available to receive the order during the delivery time slot and keep your phone switched on. Our driver will call the recipient before arrival.
  • Failed delivery: If delivery is delayed or our driver has to wait because of reasons on the customer's side (for example, we cannot reach you or no one is available to receive the order), the customer will bear the related costs. Re-arranging delivery costs an extra HK$250.
  • Same-day orders: Please place your order first, then contact our customer service team on WhatsApp straight away. We will do our best to arrange same-day catering, depending on availability that day.

Bad Weather Arrangements: Typhoon Signal No. 8 and Black Rainstorm Warning

  • Black Rainstorm Warning / Typhoon Signal No. 8: If either is in force within four hours before your delivery time, you can reschedule free of charge.
  • Typhoon Signal No. 3 / Red Rainstorm Warning: Deliveries go ahead as usual. If the Hong Kong Observatory announces that Signal No. 3 will be issued, same-day orders can be rescheduled free of charge up to four hours before the food is prepared.
  • After a signal is cancelled: Delivery service resumes five hours after the signal is cancelled. If your delivery time falls within these five hours and you have not been notified by our customer service team, the order will be treated as cancelled. Please wait for our team to contact you to reschedule.
  • Rescheduling period: Rescheduled orders must be delivered within 60 days (excluding public holidays and their eves). If no new delivery date is arranged within this period, the set will expire.

Catering Delivery FAQ

How much is catering delivery in Hong Kong?
Delivery fees are charged by district: from HK$70 in Kowloon, from HK$60 in the New Territories and from HK$180 on Hong Kong Island. Please see the fee tables on this page for each area.
How much do I need to spend for free catering delivery?
For a single order of HK$700 or more, delivery is free to selected Kowloon areas and half price for Hong Kong Island and the New Territories. For a single order of HK$1,450 or more, delivery is free across Hong Kong (excluding areas we do not deliver to). The discount is calculated automatically, with no promo code needed.
Can I order same-day catering?
Yes. Please place your order first, then contact our customer service team on WhatsApp straight away. We will do our best to arrange delivery, depending on availability that day.
Can I choose a specific delivery time?
Yes. You can choose an exact delivery time within standard delivery hours (11:00 am to 8:30 pm), with a ±15-minute window, for an extra HK$200. Surcharges apply to deliveries outside standard hours.
Do you deliver catering to the outlying islands?
We do not currently deliver to the outlying islands or the Frontier Closed Area. Tung Chung and the Airport are within our delivery area.
What happens to my catering order if Typhoon Signal No. 8 or a Black Rainstorm Warning is issued?
If a Black Rainstorm Warning or Typhoon Signal No. 8 is in force within four hours before your delivery time, you can reschedule free of charge. The new delivery must take place within 60 days (excluding public holidays and their eves).
