Planning an Annual Dinner in Hong Kong: A Catering Checklist

If you are the annual dinner planner for your company, sort out the catering early so the rest of the evening runs smoothly:

  • Headcount and budget: confirm guest numbers and budget per person first. Our annual dinner menus start from HK$300 per person.
  • Venue and set-up space: check how much space the venue has for setting up the food, and whether guests will be seated or standing.
  • Timing: fix the start time of dinner, then arrange the delivery time around it.
  • Dietary needs: collect vegetarian and other dietary requirements in advance.
  • Service staff: if you need staff on site, let us know when you enquire.

Corporate Catering FAQ

How much does corporate catering cost?
It depends on headcount, menu and delivery area. As a reference, office lunch commercial lunch boxes start from HK$108 per person, meeting lunch executive meal boxes from HK$248 per person, and annual dinners, year-end dinners and company gatherings from HK$300 per person.
How far in advance should I book corporate catering?
We recommend booking as early as possible, especially during the busy company event season from December to Chinese New Year. The lead time depends on headcount and menu, so please check with our team on WhatsApp first.
Do you cater year-end dinners and spring dinners?
Yes. Annual dinners, year-end dinners and Chinese New Year spring dinners usually include welcome canapés, hot mains and desserts, from HK$300 per person. December to February is peak season, so we recommend booking early.
Can schools, hospitals and NGOs order catering?
Yes. For school, hospital and NGO events, we can arrange meal boxes, buffets or snack platters to suit your headcount, venue and budget. Send us your event details for a quotation.
Do you offer vegetarian options for corporate catering?
Yes. Tell us how many vegetarian guests you have and their requirements, such as vegan or no five pungent spices, and we will arrange the menu accordingly.
What budget do I need for annual dinner catering?
From HK$300 per person, annual dinner catering for 100 guests costs about HK$30,000, including welcome canapés, mains and desserts.
