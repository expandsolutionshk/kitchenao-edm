Dish Details

Slow-Cooked Red Beancurd Whole Yellow Chicken

Ingredient: Premium local yellow chicken, tender with a natural depth of flavour, served in its own wooden box.
Marinade: Red beancurd blends with the natural fat under the skin. The chicken is brined first so it stays moist and seasoned throughout.
Cooking: Slow-cooked at low temperature for even doneness, then roasted at high heat to render the fat. Golden, crisp, caramelised skin with juicy, never-dry meat.
Festive sauce: Served with a Christmas rose Campari sauce whose sweet-tart fruitiness cuts through the richness.

Treasure Box Smoked Slow-Cooked US Beef Chuck Flap

Rare cut: The chuck flap tail (known in Japanese yakiniku as "zabuton") comes from a little-used part of the shoulder. Each animal yields only about 2 to 3 kg.
Texture: Fine, even marbling with almost no sinew. Tender and juicy with a rich beefy aroma and a satisfying bite, never greasy.
Cooking: Slow-cooked precisely to medium, then seared over high heat with smoking wood for a smoky, caramelised crust that locks in the juices.
Limited edition: Served in a vintage box that steals the show the moment it opens. Only 100 boxes in Hong Kong. The beef is wrapped in parchment to keep it warm and hygienic in transit.

Mango & Chestnut Winter Puff Pastry Cups

Winter flavour: Japanese chestnuts blended into a rich, sweet purée with a dense, velvety texture.
Sweet and tangy: Topped with fresh mango cubes that lighten the chestnut and open the palate as a starter.

Mentaiko Cream Cheese Croissants

Japanese flavour: Mentaiko folded into smooth cream cheese, gently savoury with a hint of sweetness.
Flaky texture: Croissants baked golden and crisp, with buttery layers that contrast the creamy filling.
Party pick: Bite-sized, easy to pick up and good served at room temperature, ideal as a starter.

Spanish Chorizo Lava Cheese Cups

Chorizo: Imported Spanish salami, bold and full of flavour.
Three-cheese lava: Cheddar, Parmesan and stretchy Mozzarella baked together.
Rich harmony: The chorizo oils melt into the cheese, whose creaminess tames the heat. Best enjoyed hot.

Ruby Pomegranate Salad Series

Festive star: Ruby-red pomegranate seeds give a jewel-like Christmas look.
Fresh and light: Naturally sweet-tart pomegranate refreshes the palate and balances richer dishes.
Two choices: Two separate salads, chosen within the set to suit different diets:
· Pomegranate Prawn & Pomelo Salad with Yuzu Lemon Vinaigrette
· Pomegranate Smoked Turkey Breast & Peach Salad with Cucumber Yogurt Dressing

King Crab Stick, Crab Meat, Mango & Avocado Salad with Lemon Aioli

Double sweetness: Crab meat and king crab sticks with sweet mango and creamy avocado.
Fresh and smooth: Lifted by lemon aioli, bright and refreshing against the richer Christmas dishes.

Double Lobster Three-Cheese Claypot Rice

Oozing burrata: A whole burrata on top reveals a creamy centre when cut, with a delicate milky aroma.
Double lobster: Rice soaked in intense lobster bisque, topped with sweet lobster meat for seafood and cheese in every bite.
Perfect balance: Once mixed, the fresh, milky burrata softens the bold lobster flavour for silky, glossy rice.

Rosé Gochujang Kimchi Prawn Risotto

Korean trend: Built on trending Korean rosé sauce, a blend of gochujang and cream.
Balanced flavour: Cooked with kimchi, the cream mellows its sharp heat.
Bold taste: Sweet with a gentle kick, a creamy, appetising twist on classic risotto.

Christmas Reindeer Crunchy Pistachio Chocolate Tarts

Pistachio white chocolate filling: Pistachio white chocolate cream mixed with crunchy pearls, finished with toasted chopped pistachios.
Reindeer design: Chocolate cookie antlers on top, as if popping a little reindeer into your mouth.
Light and festive: Light and never cloying, yet full of Christmas cheer. The perfect sweet ending to a party.

Christmas Tree Cheese & Cold Cut Platter (Shiny Cold Cut Tree-style Platter)

Three cured meats, three textures: Coppa is tender with fine marbling that melts in the mouth; Salame is firm and springy; Pepperoni is dry with a satisfying chew. Soft, firm and chewy in one platter, so every bite stays interesting.
Flavours in balance: Coppa brings a mellow, elegant richness; Salame offers classic savoury notes of garlic and black pepper; Pepperoni adds a lively kick of chilli and paprika. A slice of Pepperoni between the richer Coppa and Salame refreshes the palate, so the platter only gets better.
A feast of colour: Marbled Coppa, Salame flecked with fat and black pepper, and bright red-orange Pepperoni make a vibrant, appetising spread.
A 3D Christmas tree: The three meats are arranged with snowball-like mini buffalo mozzarella, fresh fruit and olives into a standing Christmas tree. The instant centrepiece that makes you the star of the party, perfect with red or white wine.
