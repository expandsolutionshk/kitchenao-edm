FAQ

聖誕到會常見問題

聖誕到會要提早幾多日預訂？

平安夜及聖誕節是全年到會高峰，熱門送貨時段較快額滿，建議盡早預訂。2026 年 11 月 30 日 23:59 前輸入優惠碼 MEGA79，聖誕套餐更可享七九折。

聖誕套餐甚麼日子供應？

聖誕套餐於 2026 年 12 月 12 日至 12 月 27 日供應及送貨。12 月 19 日前，其他套餐及單點照常供應；除企業訂單外，其他日子不供應聖誕套餐。供應情況會持續於網站更新，請留意送貨需知。

平安夜及聖誕節可以送貨嗎？

可以按落單頁面提供的日期及時段送貨。平安夜（12 月 24 日）及聖誕節（12 月 25 日）時段有限，建議預留至少 30 分鐘緩衝時間，詳情可參考送貨需知。

聖誕到會套餐每位大約幾錢？

Kitchen AO 2026 聖誕套餐以原價計，每位約 HK$226 至 386，人數愈多每位愈低；11 月 30 日前以 MEGA79 再享七九折。

聖誕大餐吃什麼？

一桌完整的聖誕大餐一般包括一至兩款焦點主菜（例如原隻燒雞或燒牛肉）、前菜、沙律、意粉或飯，以及甜品。Kitchen AO 的聖誕大餐到會套餐已按人數配搭好以上各類菜式。

聖誕套餐有沒有火雞？

2026 年聖誕套餐的主菜為燒南乳香慢煮原隻黃油雞及寶箱煙燻慢煮美國牛翼板肉。如有其他主菜需要，歡迎 WhatsApp 查詢。

10 人或 20 人聖誕派對應揀哪個套餐？

10 人可選 8-10 人套餐；20 人可選 18-22 人套餐。人數介乎兩個套餐之間時，可選較大的套餐，或選較小的套餐再加配單點小食，詳見上方「按人數揀聖誕套餐」。

聖誕套餐的菜式可以更換嗎？

套餐菜式可按喜好自由配搭；部分精選菜式需另加差價，實際價錢以落單頁面選項為準。

可以加素食或兒童菜式嗎？

可以在套餐以外加配單點小食及甜品，素食朋友亦可選擇「素」味人生素食到會套餐。

聖誕 party 到會可以送到 Party Room 或辦公室嗎？

可以。聖誕到會外賣會送到指定地址樓下或車輛可停泊的位置；Party Room、辦公室及學校請先確認收貨安排。無論是聖誕外賣到會還是聖誕套餐到會，都建議預留至少 30 分鐘緩衝時間。

公司聖誕派對可以索取報價嗎？

可以。公司、學校及機構訂購如需正式報價及收據，可經到會報價查詢聯絡我們。

聖誕到會包括餐具嗎？

落單時可選擇是否需要碟及餐具，亦可加配食物夾等用品。

菜式詳細介紹

燒南乳香慢煮原隻黃油雞

嚴選食材：選用本地優質黃油雞，肉質鮮嫩，富天然雞香，以專屬木箱盛載。
惹味醃製：以南乳醬與雞皮下天然脂肪的風味互相交融。烹調前先以鹽水浸漬（Brining），令肉質充分吸收水分並均勻入味。
烹調工藝：先低溫慢煮確保熟度均勻，最後以高溫烤箱炙燒逼出皮下脂肪。外皮烤至金黃酥脆的焦糖色澤，內裡肉汁完整鎖住，雞肉鮮嫩不乾柴。
節日佐料：配上充滿聖誕氣氛的「聖誕玫瑰金巴利汁」，酸甜果香中和油膩感，風味層次更豐富。

寶箱煙燻慢煮美國牛翼板肉

稀有部位：嚴選牛肩胛部（Chuck）極少活動的「下肩胛翼板肉」，美規稱 Chuck Flap Tail，日式燒肉稱「羽下肉」或「座布團」。每頭牛僅能切出約 2 至 3 公斤，屬極珍貴的精修部位。
口感風味：油花呈細密的大理石網狀分布，幾乎無筋。入口軟嫩多汁，帶濃郁牛油香氣，同時保留適度肉感嚼勁，肥而不膩。
匠心烹調：牛肉先以慢煮方式精準烹調至五成熟，再以大火配煙燻木料包裹炙烤。外層焦香並帶煙燻風味，內裡完美鎖住肉汁。
限量呈獻：以復古寶箱（Vintage Box）盛載，開箱一刻即成全場焦點，全港限量 100 個。箱內以牛油紙妥善包裹牛肉，兼顧運送保溫及食用衛生。

芒果粟子蓉冬日酥盒

冬日風味：以充滿聖誕氣氛的日本栗子為核心，打成香濃栗子蓉，栗香濃郁，甘甜扎實。
酸甜平衡：酥盒頂部綴以新鮮芒果粒，清爽果香平衡栗子蓉的厚重感，作為前菜開胃又富冬日層次。

明太子芝士忌廉牛角酥

日式風味：以明太子拌入香滑忌廉芝士，帶淡淡海鮮鹹香，鹹中帶甜。
酥香口感：牛角酥烘至金黃酥脆，層層牛油香氣，與柔滑餡料形成對比。
派對之選：一口份量方便拿取，冷食亦可，適合作為前菜。

西班牙辣肉腸熔岩芝士杯

精選肉腸：選用進口西班牙薩拉米（Salami）辣肉腸，惹味香濃。
三重芝士熔岩：混合車打芝士（Cheddar）、帕馬臣芝士（Parmesan）及拉絲效果出眾的馬蘇里拉芝士（Mozzarella）一同烘烤。
風味交融：肉腸溢出的油脂與三重芝士融為一體，芝士奶香中和辣度，香濃而不嗆口，最宜趁熱享用。

紅寶神石榴沙律系列

節日主角：以猶如紅寶石般閃亮的「紅寶神石榴」為聖誕主題核心，外觀耀眼，節日氣氛滿分。
清新解膩：石榴天然酸甜，醒胃提味，平衡聖誕派對中的濃味菜式。
兩款任選：兩款沙律為不同單點，可於套餐內按喜好選擇，照顧不同賓客的飲食需要：
・紅寶神石榴大蝦柚肉沙律，配清新酸甜的柚子檸檬油醋汁
・紅寶神石榴煙火雞胸蜜桃沙律，配清爽健康的青瓜乳酪醬

皇帝蟹棒蟹肉芒果牛油果沙律，檸檬蛋黃醬

雙重鮮甜：蟹肉與皇帝蟹棒雙重鮮甜，配搭香甜芒果及牛油果的乳脂香。
清爽順滑：以檸檬蛋黃醬提味，酸香清新，平衡聖誕派對中的濃味菜式。

雙重龍蝦三重芝士煲仔鍋飯

流心布袋芝士：飯面鋪上原個布袋芝士（Burrata），切開即見誘人流心效果，散發淡淡鮮奶幽香。
雙重龍蝦享受：飯底吸滿濃郁龍蝦汁，帶強烈鮮味及龍蝦殼香氣，再配鮮甜龍蝦仔肉，每一口都有豐富海鮮滋味及芝士香。
完美中和：拌勻後，布袋芝士的清香奶味恰好中和龍蝦汁的濃烈鮮味，令飯粒更油潤順滑，層次分明。

玫瑰醬泡菜海蝦意大利飯

韓風熱潮：以韓國大熱的「玫瑰醬」為基底，即韓式辣醬混合忌廉。
風味平衡：加入泡菜一同烹調，忌廉奶香柔和了泡菜的刺辣感。
惹味口感：入口香甜，帶微辣提味，打破傳統意大利飯框架，香濃又開胃。

聖誕馴鹿脆脆開心果朱古力撻

開心果白朱古力餡：開心果白朱古力醬混入香脆脆片，面層再鋪上烘香開心果碎，香滑與香脆雙重滿足。
馴鹿造型：頂部以朱古力味曲奇製成鹿角，造型可愛，猶如一口把馴鹿放進口中。
輕盈應節：口感輕盈不膩，同時充滿聖誕氣氛，是派對尾聲的理想甜品。

聖誕樹芝士火腿拼盤（Shiny Cold Cut Tree-style Platter）

三款火腿．三種口感：Coppa 脂肪細緻、口感軟嫩，入口即化；Salame 質地紮實有彈性；Pepperoni 乾爽而有嚼勁。軟嫩、紮實、耐嚼三種口感一次過品嚐，越吃越有層次。
風味互相平衡：Coppa 油脂香氣溫潤高雅；Salame 帶經典鹹香及大蒜黑胡椒風味；Pepperoni 則有鮮明辣味及紅椒香。在 Coppa 和 Salame 的濃郁油脂之間來一片微辣 Pepperoni，即時解膩開胃，整盤冷肉越吃越順口。
色彩繽紛：Coppa 帶大理石般的油花，Salame 有細碎白色脂肪粒及黑椒點綴，Pepperoni 呈鮮豔紅橙色，擺在一起色彩豐富，令人食指大動。
立體聖誕樹：三款火腿配上雪球般的迷你水牛芝士，再以生果及橄欖點綴，砌成立體聖誕樹，一上桌即成全場焦點，讓你成為派對主角。配紅白酒更佳。

FAQ

Christmas Catering FAQ

How far in advance should I book Christmas catering?

Christmas Eve and Christmas Day are the busiest catering days of the year and popular delivery slots fill up quickly, so we recommend booking early. Enter code MEGA79 before 30 Nov 2026, 23:59 for 21% off Christmas sets.

When are the Christmas sets available?

Christmas sets are available for delivery from 12 to 27 December 2026. Other sets and à la carte items are available as usual until 19 December. Apart from corporate orders, Christmas sets are not offered on other dates. Availability will be updated on our website, so please check our delivery information.

Do you deliver on Christmas Eve and Christmas Day?

Yes, on the dates and time slots shown on the order page. Slots on Christmas Eve (24 Dec) and Christmas Day (25 Dec) are limited. Please allow at least 30 minutes of buffer time and see our delivery information for details.

How much does Christmas catering cost per person?

At regular prices, Kitchen AO 2026 Christmas sets cost about HK$226 to 386 per person, and less per person for larger groups. Enter MEGA79 before 30 Nov for a further 21% off.

What should I serve for Christmas dinner?

A complete Christmas dinner usually includes one or two showpiece mains (such as a whole roast chicken or roast beef), starters, salad, pasta or rice, and dessert. Kitchen AO Christmas catering sets include all of these, portioned by headcount.

Do your Christmas sets include turkey?

Our 2026 Christmas mains are a slow-cooked red beancurd whole yellow chicken and smoked slow-cooked US beef chuck flap in a treasure box. For other mains, please ask us on WhatsApp.

Which set should I choose for 10 or 20 guests?

For 10 guests, choose the 8-10 pax set. For 20 guests, choose the 18-22 pax set. If your headcount falls between two sets, choose the larger set, or the smaller set plus à la carte snacks. See "Choose a Christmas Set by Headcount" above.

Can I change the dishes in a Christmas set?

Dishes can be mixed and matched to your taste. Some premium dishes carry a surcharge, shown on the order page.

Can I add vegetarian or children's dishes?

Yes. You can add à la carte snacks and desserts to any set, and vegetarian guests can choose our vegetarian catering sets.

Can Christmas party catering be delivered to a party room or office?

Yes. Christmas catering is delivered to the ground floor of your address or wherever the vehicle can park. For party rooms, offices and schools, please confirm receiving arrangements in advance, and allow at least 30 minutes of buffer time.

Can I get a quotation for an office Christmas party?

Yes. Companies, schools and organisations needing a formal quotation and receipt can contact us through our catering quote enquiry form.

Is cutlery included?

You can choose plates and cutlery when ordering, and add serving tongs and other supplies.

Dish Details

Slow-Cooked Red Beancurd Whole Yellow Chicken

Ingredient: Premium local yellow chicken, tender with a natural depth of flavour, served in its own wooden box.
Marinade: Red beancurd blends with the natural fat under the skin. The chicken is brined first so it stays moist and seasoned throughout.
Cooking: Slow-cooked at low temperature for even doneness, then roasted at high heat to render the fat. Golden, crisp, caramelised skin with juicy, never-dry meat.
Festive sauce: Served with a Christmas rose Campari sauce whose sweet-tart fruitiness cuts through the richness.

Treasure Box Smoked Slow-Cooked US Beef Chuck Flap

Rare cut: The chuck flap tail (known in Japanese yakiniku as "zabuton") comes from a little-used part of the shoulder. Each animal yields only about 2 to 3 kg.
Texture: Fine, even marbling with almost no sinew. Tender and juicy with a rich beefy aroma and a satisfying bite, never greasy.
Cooking: Slow-cooked precisely to medium, then seared over high heat with smoking wood for a smoky, caramelised crust that locks in the juices.
Limited edition: Served in a vintage box that steals the show the moment it opens. Only 100 boxes in Hong Kong. The beef is wrapped in parchment to keep it warm and hygienic in transit.

Mango & Chestnut Winter Puff Pastry Cups

Winter flavour: Japanese chestnuts blended into a rich, sweet purée with a dense, velvety texture.
Sweet and tangy: Topped with fresh mango cubes that lighten the chestnut and open the palate as a starter.

Mentaiko Cream Cheese Croissants

Japanese flavour: Mentaiko folded into smooth cream cheese, gently savoury with a hint of sweetness.
Flaky texture: Croissants baked golden and crisp, with buttery layers that contrast the creamy filling.
Party pick: Bite-sized, easy to pick up and good served at room temperature, ideal as a starter.
